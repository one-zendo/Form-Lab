# Duo hardware preview standard

Canonical schematic SVGs: `design/duo/FP.svg`, `FL1.svg`, `FL2.svg`, `UP.svg`, `UL.svg`. Silver-gray chassis, thin black bezel, circular camera, asymmetric folded shell. FL1 is the base folded landscape drawing; FL2 rotates 180 degrees; FP rotates 90 degrees. Hinge bottom/top/left respectively. Display units match reference dimensions in DUO_REFERENCE.md; render at equal scale. Corner radii, chassis thickness and camera coordinates are illustrative, not measured; outer camera offset provisional; inner camera is under-display and has no permanent visible cutout. These are design-only assets, never loaded by the app. Compose UI separately inside display bounds, with outer camera over UI and safe areas from actual runtime. Do not subtract browser-excluded strips again. Native full display access remains unverified.

Fixtures in tests/fixtures/duo-orientations.json are regression data, not runtime breakpoints. See DUO_REFERENCE.md for measurement provenance.

## Official hardware reference
Apple https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphone-duo-iphde2xz6efm/ios confirms outer camera at top right in portrait and inner under-display camera. https://www.apple.com/ca/iphone-duo/specs/ confirms display resolutions 1398×2034 outer, 1878×2670 inner. Existing design viewport fixtures are independent of these resolutions. Folded FL1 master camera center (29,29), radius 8 remains unverified; transformed FP center (437,29), FL2 (649,437). Do not describe these as measured. No visible cutout in UP/UL. Use exact asymmetric display path for clipping, not a generic rounded rectangle. Runtime unchanged.
